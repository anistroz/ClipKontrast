FAQ

Questions

Who are the clippers?

Creators of short videos across India who use ClipKontrast's free clip-maker. They join your campaign, make clips from your streams, and post them on their own YouTube, Instagram and X accounts.

How are views checked?

Every clip sent in is checked before anyone is paid: that it's from the clipper's own account, posted after the campaign started, with the right tag. YouTube views are read from YouTube itself; Instagram and X views from the post's insights. Clips that break the rules or show fake views are rejected.

Is the ClipKontrast app still free?

Yes. The ClipKontrast clip maker is free for everyone who clips, and it stays free. Campaigns are paid by the creators and brands who launch them, and that money goes to the clippers who make the clips.

What's the minimum budget?

Campaigns start at ₹1,00,000.

Do I need to do anything after launch?

No. We run the campaign and keep you posted on clips and views. You can send us new streams and moments any time.
